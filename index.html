Structural Engineering

Multi-Layer BOPP Woven Sack Architecture

Our flagship BOPP laminated bag is an advanced engineered laminate fusing tensile drop resistance with photorealistic shelf presence. Click below to inspect the structural layers:

01

Reverse-Printed BOPP Film (12–18µ)

10-color HD rotogravure trapped between film and laminate, making graphics 100% scratch-proof and UV resistant.

02

Corona-Treated Extrusion Tie-Layer (15–20 GSM)

Homogeneous molten PP polymer bonding film directly to the woven fabric with zero risk of delamination or pinholes.

03

Circular Woven High-Tenacity PP Fabric (55–130 GSM)

Micro-denier virgin PP tape woven on high-speed circular looms for extraordinary burst pressure and drop shock resilience.

04

Optional LDPE / Barrier Inner Liner

Hermetic barrier providing 100% moisture sealing, aroma preservation, and oil resistance for food & agrochemicals.

Procurement FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding custom dimensions, cylinder lead times, MOQs, and global export shipping terms.

What types of BOPP woven bags do you manufacture? +
We manufacture single-side and both-side BOPP laminated bags, side gusseted sacks, block-bottom valve sacks, pinch bottom bags, bags with ultrasonic punched D-cut handles or plastic handles, micro-perforated bags, transparent window bags, and metallic/matte finish laminated bags.
Can the bags be fully customized for dimensions, GSM, and printing? +
Yes. Every bag is tailored to client requirements including width (30cm to 85cm), length, mesh count (8x8 to 14x14), fabric GSM (55 to 140 GSM), denier (600D to 1200D), and up to 10-color reverse rotogravure printing.
What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and production lead time? +
For custom rotogravure printed BOPP bags, our standard MOQ is 20,000 to 30,000 bags (approx. 1x20' FCL). New custom orders are manufactured within 3 to 4 weeks (including cylinder engraving), while repeat orders are ready for port dispatch within 10 to 14 business days.
Do you provide food-grade certifications for rice, flour, and sugar? +
Yes. We are certified under ISO 22000 and BRCGS Global Standard for Packaging Materials. All food bags utilize 100% virgin food-grade polymer and compliant inks meeting FDA (21 CFR) and EU Regulation 10/2011 standards.
How can I request a formal quotation or physical sample kit? +
You can submit your bag dimensions and artwork via our online RFQ modal or email our export desk at export@vardhmanpolyfab.com. We provide comprehensive technical proposals within 12 business hours and dispatch express physical sample kits worldwide.
