The Science of Packaging

From Prime Polymer to Photorealistic Protection

Every sack engineered at our 250,000 sq. ft. campus is a multi-layer composite designed to withstand heavy mechanical drop shock while presenting brilliant shelf appeal.

Reverse-Printed BOPP Shield

Corona-treated 12–18µ film with reverse ink trapping prevents scuffing, water exposure, and ink migration.

High-Tenacity Circular Woven Fabric

High denier virgin polypropylene tapes woven seamlessly without longitudinal seams for maximum load containment.

Tandem Extrusion Lamination

Bubble-free molten PP tie-layer creates a permanent hermetic bond impervious to moisture and gas penetration.
