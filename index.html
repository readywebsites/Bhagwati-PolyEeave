About Vardhman Polyfab

World-Class Woven Packaging Solutions Built for Global Logistics

Established in 1999, Vardhman Polyfab Industries Ltd. has grown to become one of India's premier vertically integrated manufacturers and exporters of high-performance Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene (BOPP) laminated woven bags and polypropylene woven sacks.

Operating from a 250,000 sq. ft. modern facility in Gujarat with state-of-the-art European tape extrusion lines, circular looms, and high-precision 10-color rotogravure printing presses, we engineer packaging that provides unparalleled protection, barrier properties, and shelf appeal for world-leading brands.

Manufacturing Excellence

End-to-End Production Process

Our integrated 250,000 sq. ft. plant transforms premium polypropylene granules into finished, export-ready BOPP woven bags under stringent quality control.

01

Raw Material Polymer

100% prime virgin PP polymer resin and UV masterbatch inspected for Melt Flow Index (MFI).

02

Tape Extrusion

High-speed extrusion line producing precision micro-denier tapes with high tensile tenacity.

03

Circular Weaving

Advanced 6-shuttle & 8-shuttle circular looms weaving tubular seamless fabrics with zero warp defects.

04

BOPP Lamination

Tandem extrusion lamination bonding reverse-printed BOPP film to woven fabric with high peel strength.

05

Rotogravure Printing

Up to 10-color high-speed gravure printing cylinders delivering photo-quality graphics and barcode fidelity.

06

Cutting & Stitching

Automated bag conversion lines with optical sensors for precision hot/cold cutting, hem stitching, and handles.

07

Quality Inspection

100% dimensional, drop test, burst pressure, tensile elongation, and color registration verification.

08

Baling & Dispatch

Hydraulic bale pressing, palletizing with stretch film wrapping, and container loading for global export.

Procurement FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our bag manufacturing specifications, MOQs, printing capabilities, and export terms.

What types of BOPP woven bags do you manufacture? +
We manufacture single-side and both-side BOPP laminated bags, side gusseted bags, block-bottom valve sacks, pinch bottom bags, bags with ultrasonic handles or punched D-cut handles, micro-perforated bags, transparent window bags, and metallic/matte finish laminated bags.
Can the bags be fully customized for dimensions, printing, and GSM? +
Yes. Every bag we produce is fully customized to client specifications including width (30cm to 85cm), length, mesh count (8x8 to 14x14), fabric GSM (55 to 140 GSM), denier (600D to 1200D), and up to 10-color reverse rotogravure printing.
What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +
For custom rotogravure printed BOPP bags, our standard MOQ is 20,000 to 30,000 bags (equivalent to one 20ft container FCL or combined order) depending on cylinder dimensions and size. For unprinted or standard flexo bags, MOQs start from 10,000 pcs.
What is your production and export lead time? +
For new custom printed orders (including cylinder engraving and proofing), typical production lead time is 3 to 4 weeks. Repeat orders are manufactured and ready for port dispatch within 10 to 14 business days.
Do you provide food-grade certification for rice, flour, and sugar bags? +
Yes. We are certified under ISO 22000 and BRCGS Global Standard for Packaging Materials. Our food bags use 100% virgin food-grade virgin polymer and compliant inks meeting FDA (21 CFR) and EU Regulation 10/2011 standards.
How can I request a formal quotation or physical sample? +
You can submit your bag dimensions, target capacity, and artwork requirements via our online RFQ form, or email our export sales team directly at export@vardhmanpolyfab.com. We provide technical quotation proposals within 12 business hours and express courier sample kits worldwide.
