BOPP Laminated Woven Rice & Grain Bags

High-tenacity polypropylene woven fabric laminated with reverse rotogravure printed BOPP film, engineered for premium supermarket shelf impact, moisture barrier, and zero transit rupture.

Standard Certifications

BRCGS Food Grade ISO 9001:2015 FDA 21 CFR Compliant 100% Recyclable PP
Flagship Packaging Line

Premium BOPP Laminated Rice & Grain Sacks

Designed specifically for premium Basmati rice, specialty grains, flour, and organic pulses, our BOPP laminated woven sacks provide photorealistic visual branding while offering rugged structural integrity that eliminates bag bursting during intermodal ocean transit and pallet handling.

Technical Parameters

Parameter Specification Standard
Base Fabric 100% Prime Virgin Polypropylene (Circular Woven Tubular)
Lamination Film Corona-Treated BOPP Film (12 to 18 Microns, Glossy / Matte)
Fabric GSM 55 GSM to 135 GSM (Customizable per capacity load)
Tape Denier / Mesh 600 D to 1100 D • Mesh count: 9x9 to 14x14 tapes/inch
Bag Width 30 cm to 85 cm (12 inches to 33.5 inches)
Bag Length 40 cm to 130 cm (15.7 inches to 51.2 inches)
Printing Capacity Up to 10 Colors High-Speed Reverse Rotogravure Printing
Top Finish Heat cut, ultrasonic hemmed, easy-open pull tape, top handle
Bottom Finish Single / Double folded with single / double chain stitching
Gusseting Side gusset (5 cm to 15 cm) with continuous side print
Air Venting Micro-perforation / laser pin-hole technology for air degassing
Target Capacity 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg, 25 kg, 40 kg, 50 kg
