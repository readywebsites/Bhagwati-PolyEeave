Packaging Engineered for Every Industry

From farm fields and chemical reactors to retail supermarket shelves, our woven packaging lines are specifically optimized for product weight, atmospheric exposure, automated filling speeds, and logistics handling.

Comprehensive Industry Solutions

Agriculture & Seeds

Seed, Grain & Harvest Storage

Features UV stabilized polypropylene mesh with controlled airflow permeability to prevent moisture buildup and grain rotting during prolonged seasonal storage in tropical conditions.

  • ✓ UV resistance from 200 to 1,200 KLY
  • ✓ High puncture resistance for sharp seed kernels
  • ✓ Standard 25kg, 40kg & 50kg capacities
Food Grade

Basmati Rice, Sugar & Wheat Flour

Manufactured in our BRCGS certified clean-room using 100% virgin polymer resin. High-definition 10-color reverse rotogravure printing elevates consumer shelf appeal in supermarkets.

  • ✓ FDA 21 CFR and EU Food Contact compliant
  • ✓ 100% moisture barrier preventing sugar caking
  • ✓ Ultrasonic welded handles & transparent window
Building Materials

Cement, Wall Putty & Dry Mortar

Block-bottom valve sacks engineered with Austrian hot-air welding technology. Designed for high-speed automated packing rotors with rapid micro-pore de-aeration and zero dust leakage.

  • ✓ Perfect brick shape for 40-bag automated palletizing
  • ✓ High drop burst strength resisting 2.5m drop
  • ✓ Self-closing internal valve sleeve
Heavy Industry

Agro Fertilizers & Chemical Resins

Robust woven sacks with extrusion lamination and optional loose/stitched LDPE inner liners to protect hygroscopic urea, NPK, potash, and caustic polymer granules.

  • ✓ Corrosion-resistant multi-layer barrier film
  • ✓ Micro-perforations for degassing ammonia fumes
  • ✓ Reinforced heavy-gauge bottom stitching
