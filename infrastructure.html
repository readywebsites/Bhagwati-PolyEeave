State-of-the-Art Plant

World-Class Manufacturing Infrastructure

Spanning 250,000 sq. ft. of automated manufacturing in Gujarat, India, our integrated facility combines European extrusion technology, high-speed looms, and 10-color electronic gravure printing.

Facility Overview

Scale, Automation & Precision Engineering

Designed from the ground up to support high-volume export contracts, our plant operates 24/7 under computerized process control, automated pneumatic resin conveying, and HEPA-filtered clean room environments for food-grade packaging.

Our vertical integration ensures that raw virgin polymer entering our gate is converted into precision slit tape, woven into seamless tubular fabric, laminated with photorealistic printed BOPP film, and converted into finished bags under a single continuous quality oversight system.
