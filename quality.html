Testing Protocol

Our 8-Stage Quality Assurance Cycle

Rigorous checkpoints verifying structural integrity, dimensional tolerance, barrier properties, and optical fidelity at each milestone.

01

Resin & MFI Audit

Melt Flow Index (MFI), density gradient, moisture content, and masterbatch color dispersion verified per ASTM D1238.

02

Tape Tenacity & Denier

Tensile strength testing of individual slit PP tapes ensuring minimum 5.5 to 6.2 g/denier tenacity and controlled elongation.

03

GSM & Mesh Density

Round cutter fabric sample weighing on analytical microbalances and optical thread count verification per square inch.

04

Corona Dyne Testing

Dyne test pen inspection confirming surface treatment above 40 dynes/cm for permanent ink adhesion and zero film flaking.

05

Lamination Peel Test

180° tensile peel testing measuring adhesion bond strength between reverse-printed BOPP film and woven polypropylene substrate.

06

Drop & Burst Resistance

Filled bags subjected to 2.5-meter free-fall drop tower impacts on flat face, side gusset, and bottom seams per ASTM D5276.

07

Accelerated UV Test

QUV weatherometer exposure testing simulating 1,200 hours of aggressive UV radiation and condensation with >80% retained strength.

08

Pre-Dispatch Audit

100% final inspection for seam stitch tension, handle weld pull resistance, barcode scannability (Grade A), and pallet moisture seals.
