Our Heritage

From Regional Pioneer to Global Packaging Exporter

Founded in 1999 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Vardhman Polyfab Industries began with a single high-tenacity tape extrusion line and a vision to elevate Indian technical woven packaging to international quality benchmarks.

Over 25 years of relentless technological modernization, we have developed a vertically integrated manufacturing campus spanning 250,000 sq. ft., equipped with cutting-edge European extrusion lines, 120+ circular weaving looms, 10-color high-definition rotogravure printing presses, and automated conversion machinery.

Today, Vardhman Polyfab stands as a trusted manufacturing partner to Fortune 500 corporations, agricultural conglomerates, national food boards, and global cement majors across more than 50 nations.

Journey of Growth

25 Years of Manufacturing Milestones

Key milestones that defined our expansion from an initial tape extruder to a multi-plant export powerhouse.

1999

Company Founded

Operations commence in Gujarat with 1 extrusion line producing industrial PP woven bags.

2006

First Export Shipment

Achieved ISO 9001 certification and commenced direct container exports to the Middle East & Africa.

2012

BOPP & Rotogravure

Commissioned high-speed 8-color electronic rotogravure printing press for photographic BOPP packaging.

2018

BRCGS Food Safety

Inaugurated dedicated clean-room manufacturing facility for food-grade rice and sugar sacks.

2021

120M Bag Capacity

Added Austrian AD*STAR block-bottom valve sack lines and expanded total annual production to 120M bags.

2024

Solar & ESG Expansion

Installed 2.5 MW solar rooftop plant, achieving 60% renewable energy footprint in daily production.
